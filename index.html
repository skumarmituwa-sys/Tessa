Project Brief & Architecture

Concept: An intentionally impractical, hilarious UX experiment where the volume slider refuses direct manual dragging. Volume rises only when you shower Bytey with increasingly frantic compliments!

📐 Sentiment & Frantic Scoring Formula

Frantic Score = (Weighted Compliments) + Exclamations (!) + (Emojis × 1.5) + (ALL CAPS ? +5 : 0)

Volume Delta: ΔV = min(35, 2 + Frantic Score × 2.2)

🗣️ Text-To-Speech (TTS) Voice Engine

Automatic Malayalam (ml-IN) & English (en-IN) voice synthesis! Bytey speaks sassy comebacks when insulted, and cute shy remarks when over-complimented.
