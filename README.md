# Bytey — Emotional Volume Slider 🎯

### Project Description
A delightfully absurd volume controller that refuses to be dragged directly. The volume increases ONLY when you type increasingly frantic, over-the-top compliments to Bytey in English or Malayalam, and drops to 0% (Muted) with a 3x speed freakout if you insult it!

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Standard operating systems give users total, cold, unfeeling control over audio volume sliders. Computers perform heavy computations all day without receiving any emotional appreciation or validation from their human users.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
Bytey — an emotionally needy retro CRT computer system that controls audio volume based strictly on emotional validation. Praise Bytey with frantic compliments to turn up the volume, or insult Bytey and watch it freak out at 3x speed, flip upside down, and mute everything in absolute protest!

## Technical Details
### Technologies/Components Used

# Features & Highlights
- **Resisting Volume Slider**: Direct mouse dragging is forbidden! Volume moves dynamically based on frantic sentiment scores.
- **Retro CRT Computer Face Avatar**: Dynamic animated monitor bezel, CRT screen, floppy drive slot, power LED, eye tracking, and facial expressions (Happy, Shy, Angry, Crying).
- **Malayalam & English Sentiment Engine**: 100% Unicode script matching for Malayalam script & Manglish compliments, insults, and apologies.
- **3x Speed Freakout & Reverse Rewind Mode**: Insults trigger a 3.0x speed audio/visual freakout that flips the UI upside-down. The upright **Reverse ⚡ (3x Speed)** button rewinds the screen back upright while speaking Malayalam out loud at 3x speed!
- **Malayalam Text-To-Speech (TTS)**: Bytey speaks out loud in Malayalam (`ml-IN`) for compliments, freakouts, crying lines, and reverse rewinds.
- **Music Engine ("Raave" & Spotify "Why Me")**: Plays synthesized Malayalam melody presets ("Raave") and streams Spotify preview audio ("Why Me" by Manhar & Richie) connected to dynamic equalizer canvas visualizers.
